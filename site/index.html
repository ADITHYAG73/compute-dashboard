THE FINE PRINT, UP FRONT

Estimates with receipts.

We estimate allocated GPU time from lifecycle events or an explicitly recorded historical rate. This is not a measure of productive work or GPU utilization.

kWh = GPU-hours × watts ÷ 1,000 × PUE
kgCO₂e = kWh × regional kgCO₂e/kWh

Nameplate power is a proxy for draw. Unknown hardware and unverified grid mappings stay unknown. The default PUE of 1.0 is a GPU-only baseline, excluding facility overhead. Carbon is a partial operational estimate, not a complete footprint.
